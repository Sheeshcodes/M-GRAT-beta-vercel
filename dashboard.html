What this tells you

Reading the stall data correctly

An early stall (page 1–2)

Usually a framing problem. The customer did not understand why the question mattered. Rewrite the section intro, not the question.

A late stall (page 4+)

Usually a detail problem. The question needs knowledge they do not have to hand. Consider adding a "skip for now" path.

One page dominates the exits

Editorial fix, not an analytics one. If ≥ 40% of exits happen on one page, the content on that page is the problem.
