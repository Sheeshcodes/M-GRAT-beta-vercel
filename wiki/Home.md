# M-GRAT Wiki

Welcome to the M-GRAT project wiki. This is the living record of what we've built, what we've changed, and why.

---

## Pages

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Changelog](Changelog) | What changed each week — new capabilities, behaviour changes, content updates, and infrastructure |
| [Decision Log](Decision-Log) | Why significant product, design, and infrastructure decisions were made |

---

## Who maintains this

Anyone on the team can edit the Wiki directly in the browser — no PR needed. The convention is:

- **Changelog** — update at the end of each week, or when a meaningful change ships
- **Decision Log** — add an entry whenever a significant call is made that affects the product, the UX, the scoring logic, or the hosting/infrastructure
