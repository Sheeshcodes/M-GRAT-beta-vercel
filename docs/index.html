Guiding the customer

Use these five steps to understand this tool and guide the next conversation with your customer.

Open the live assessment or share the self-service version, depending on how you are engaging with the customer.

Before beginning the assessment, set clear expectations:

  • This is a diagnostic, not an audit. Honest answers help reveal the most relevant growth opportunities.
  • Focus the conversation on where the customer can gain more value, rather than on evaluating past performance.
  • Set aside 10–15 minutes for the assessment. For larger organizations, complete the assessment for one operational site or business unit at a time.

Operational profile

The report turns the customer's responses into an operational profile that shows where Maximo is creating value today and where further opportunities may exist. It highlights verified value drivers, recommends paths to greater value, and identifies a practical next step based on the customer's capabilities and priorities.

Growth capabilities

The operational profile shows where the customer is currently realizing value and where additional capabilities could support further growth. It covers eight capability areas:

  • Asset data: Asset data quality, criticality, and hierarchy
  • Work management: Job plan and preventive maintenance capabilities
  • Inspections and condition capture: Inspection programs and condition data
  • Supply chain and inventory: Storeroom and inventory readiness
  • Scheduling: Schedule planning and optimization
  • Assignment and dispatch: Work assignment and dispatch
  • Reliability practices: Failure-code governance and reliability strategies
  • Condition monitoring and prediction: Condition visibility and predictive capabilities

Each capability is shown as Emerging, Growing, or Established. These ratings help guide the conversation:

  • Established: Capabilities already supporting operational value
  • Growing: Capabilities with a foundation that can be expanded
  • Emerging: Capabilities that may present new growth opportunities

Treat the ratings as conversation starters rather than final conclusions. Consider the customer's priorities, operating model, available information, and desired outcomes before identifying the most valuable opportunity.

Verified value drivers

The report highlights verified value drivers, which are capabilities already working well and contributing to the customer's operations. Use this section to:

  • Recognize the progress the customer has already made.
  • Connect existing capabilities to measurable or observable value.
  • Build confidence in the results through supporting evidence.
  • Identify strong foundations that can enable the customer's next growth opportunity.

The conversation should begin with existing value, not capability gaps. This helps position the roadmap as an extension of the customer's success, rather than a corrective plan.

The APM and FSM paths define specific routes to greater value. Each path includes a current stage and target stage, showing where the customer is today and where additional capabilities could create the most value.

  • Review the target stage narrative. Use the business outcomes and potential improvements as conversation anchors.
  • Look beyond the primary path. If the customer's results emphasize APM, review the FSM path as well, and vice versa. This can reveal opportunities beyond the immediate next step.
  • Connect the path to Maximo. Use the recommended capabilities and supporting metrics to connect each opportunity to relevant Maximo solutions.
  • Confirm the opportunity. Ask whether the path and target stage align with the customer's priorities and desired outcomes.

Explore the paths to greater value →

The action plan identifies the customer's highest-value next step and additional actions that can support continued growth. Use the plan to turn the paths to greater value into practical next steps.

  • Start with the highest-value next step. Explain why this action was recommended and connect it to the customer's priorities, target stage, and potential value.
  • Review the foundational actions. Walk through what the customer can do now, which Maximo capabilities can help, and what support may be needed.
  • Consider the additional next steps. Show how the actions build on one another and help the customer continue progressing toward the target stage.
  • Use the supporting resources. Identify the guidance, Maximo capabilities, and IBM expertise that can help the customer move forward.
  • Let the customer lead. Ask which actions would create the most value now, then shape the next steps around the customer's response.

Bring together the customer's operational profile, paths to greater value, and action plan into a clear point of view for the follow-up conversation.

  • Lead with verified value. Recognize the capabilities already creating value and use them as the foundation for the conversation.
  • Summarize the opportunity. Connect the customer's priorities to the selected path, target stage, and potential value.
  • Focus on the action plan. Be prepared to explain the highest-value next step, additional actions, and the support available to help the customer move forward.
  • Bring in the right people. Identify the customer stakeholders and IBM experts needed to validate the opportunity and support the next steps.
  • Set a clear next step. End the conversation with an agreed action, such as a follow-up workshop, technical discovery session, or deeper discussion of the recommended path.

Use the report as a shared reference before and during the conversation. Share it with the customer and relevant stakeholders to help everyone align on the opportunity and next step.
