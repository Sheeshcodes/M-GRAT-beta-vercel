# M-GRAT Promo Video — Script & Scene Breakdown
*MAS Growth Readiness Assessment Tool — Beta v3*

---

## Overview

**Video type:** Promotional / product walkthrough  
**Target length:** 90–120 seconds  
**Tone:** Confident, warm, insight-driven — not salesy  
**Audience:** IBM sellers, MAS account owners, and customer champions

### SCENE 2 — The key question moment
**Duration:** ~10 seconds  
**Visual:**  
The assessment opens. We see a single question card — full screen — from Section 2: Your maintenance operations.

**The displayed question (use this exact wording from the tool):**
> *"Asset data — which do you consistently put in practice?"*
> 
> Rows shown:
> - Asset criticality ranking *(Formally rated by business impact — safety, compliance, downtime cost)*
> - Assets in a hierarchy *(Structured parent-child links across locations, assets, and components)*
> - Classification and specifications *(Standardised classes with technical attributes)*
> - Standardised asset registry *(Consistent naming conventions, unique asset records, and a governance workflow)*
>
> Columns: **Consistently in place** · Partially / not in place · Unknown

A cursor hovers. A beat. Then it clicks **"Consistently in place"** on *Asset criticality ranking*. The row highlights.

**Narrator (V/O):**
> "It starts with a question. A real one — about where your team actually stands today, not where you'd like to be."

### SCENE 3 — Timelapse through the questionnaire
**Duration:** ~12–15 seconds  
**Visual:**  
The assessment moves quickly — blurred, speed-ramped. As questions fly past, **5 section labels** write up one by one in the upper-left corner:

```
✓ Your goals and priorities
✓ Your maintenance operations
✓ Your field service operations
✓ Your asset performance practice
✓ Your organisational appetite
```

Each label fades in as its section is "completed." The right side of the screen shows a question counter incrementing — ending at **24**.

Meanwhile, glimpsed question titles flash past (blurred/motion):
- *"What are your top strategic business objectives?"*
- *"Which of the following describe your inspection and condition capture practice?"*
- *"How far has your scheduling practice developed in Maximo?"*
- *"Which condition monitoring capabilities are active in your environment?"*
- *"Does an executive sponsor actively support this initiative?"*

**Narrator (V/O):**
> "Twenty-four questions. Five areas of practice. In minutes, you have a complete picture of where this account stands — across asset data, work management, field service, reliability, and organisational appetite to grow."

---

### SCENE 4 — The submit moment (Aha moment)
**Duration:** ~6 seconds  
**Visual:**

A brief, satisfying animation — a loading arc, then a smooth page transition.

**Narrator (V/O):**
> "Submit."

*(Beat of silence — let the click land.)*

## Production Notes

**Key visual details to get right:**
- The **5 section labels writing up in the upper-left** during the timelapse is the narrative hook — make each one land with a small checkmark animation.
- The **question counter reaching 24** in the upper-right during the timelapse gives a satisfying sense of completion.
- The **submit button click** should be a deliberate, held moment — 1–2 seconds of silence before the report reveal. This is the emotional pivot.
- The report reveal should feel **warm and generous** — not clinical. Use soft fade-ins per section, not hard cuts.
- The **product pills** in the MAS modules section are a key sell — show the contrast between "currently using" and "what comes next" visually.
- The `"Consistently in place"` click in Scene 2 should show the row highlight animation that exists in the live tool.

**Accessibility / caption note:**  
All narrator lines should have captions. Screen text that appears as animation does not need to be read aloud — it's additive.
