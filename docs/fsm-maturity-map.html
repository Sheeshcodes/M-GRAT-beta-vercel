STAGE 2 → STAGE 3

Structured Scheduling

"Optimizes crew productivity and work preparation — enriching job plans with planned materials and tools, sequencing work by dependencies, and routing technicians by geographic proximity."

Are you ready?

Forward planning extends to rolling windows with predecessor and hierarchy constraints. System-suggested dispatch based on effective availability is active. Job plans are scoped to asset classes, include hierarchies and predecessors, and link to asset data. Inspection results feed condition data and inventory is planned against work requirements.

What you gain

  • • 15–25% reduction in technician travel time and return trips via proximity routing.
  • • Significant reduction in job delays caused by missing parts, permits, or tools.
  • • Real-time effective capacity calculation accounting for leaves and active assignments.

Key moves to get there

  • → Move to rolling planning windows with predecessor constraints
  • → Enable system-suggested dispatch on effective availability
  • → Scope job plans to asset classes and hierarchies
  • → Feed inspection results into asset condition data

What you gain

Key moves to get there

"Achieves autonomous, end-to-end field workforce execution — automated cron-based optimization, GenAI point-of-work troubleshooting, and real-time field actuals driving continuous schedule refinement."

The Optimizer operates fully autonomously for routine scheduling and dispatch. Humans are approvers and exception handlers only. Strategic inventory, AIP-connected job plans, and GenAI-assisted work execution complete the full FSM maturity picture.

What you gain

  • • Up to 26% overall technician productivity gain (achieving ≥45–55% wrench time).
  • • 10–20% reduction in overtime through autonomous schedule balancing.

Key moves to get there

  • → Run continuous, AI-assisted schedule optimization
  • → Close the loop from asset health to planning and dispatch
  • → Standardize KPIs and benchmarks across all regions
  • → Embed field feedback into job-plan and reliability improvement
