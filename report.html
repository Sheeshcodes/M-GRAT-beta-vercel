Act 2 — Opportunities to increase ROI

Two expansion paths

This section recommends an expansion path along two significant journeys.

Asset Performance Management

Asset Performance Management (APM) uses real-time monitoring and predictive analytics to eliminate unplanned downtime, cut maintenance costs, and extend asset life. It replaces rigid schedules with condition-driven actions, optimizing resource allocation, workplace safety, and long-term capital investments.

Field Service Management

Field Service Management (FSM) optimises how work is planned, assigned, and executed across your field workforce. It replaces reactive dispatch and manual scheduling with coordinated, data-driven operations — reducing technician travel time, improving first-time fix rates, and giving dispatchers real-time visibility across active jobs.
